### Bug report
Title: After capture tasks delayed until all editors closed

I often have multiple Editors open at once from multiple screenshots. Ordinarily, when I hit Continue, the screenshot is copied to the clipboard and it's saved to my screenshot folder. But sometimes I hit Continue and nothing happens. It's not in the saved image folder and it's not in the clipboard. However, if I Continue or Cancel out of all active Editors, all of the after capture tasks will finally process.

It doesn't happen every time but it has happened repeatedly since the new Editor was released. I'm currently on 20.2.
