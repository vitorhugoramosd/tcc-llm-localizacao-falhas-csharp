### Bug report
Title: ChatGPT Shopping feed exports 'customer enters price' (donation) products with price '0.00 USD'

DESCRIPTION
    Products with 'Customer enters price' enabled have Price=0 in the database. The feed writes
      price '0.00 USD' for them (sample products 'If You Wait (donation)' and 'Science & Faith'), so
      ChatGPT Shopping would show them as free. The plugin source has no handling for
      CustomerEntersPrice or CallForPrice.

STEPS TO REPRODUCE
    1. Install the ChatGPT Shopping plugin
    2. Open its Configure page and click 'Generate feed'
    3. Decompress files/exportimport/1-chatgptshopping_products.jsonl.gz
    4. Find item_id IF_YOU_WT and SCI_FAITH

EXPECTED RESULT
    These products are exported with their minimum customer-entered price
      (0.50).

ACTUAL RESULT
    Both exported with "price": "0.00 USD".

EVIDENCE
    --- text evidence: evidence\BUG-chatgpt-feed-customer-price-zero.txt ---
    | DB (Product table): IF_YOU_WT  'If You Wait (donation)'  Price=0  CustomerEntersPrice=1 MinimumCustomerEnteredPrice=0.5
    | DB (Product table): SCI_FAITH  'Science & Faith'        Price=0  CustomerEntersPrice=1 MinimumCustomerEnteredPrice=0.5
    | 
    | Feed lines (1-chatgptshopping_products.jsonl.gz):
    | {"item_id": "IF_YOU_WT", "title": "If You Wait (donation)", "price": "0.00 USD", "availability": "in_stock", "is_eligible_checkout": false}
    | {"item_id": "SCI_FAITH", "title": "Science & Faith", "price": "0.00 USD", "availability": "in_stock", "is_eligible_checkout": false}
    --- end of text evidence ---
